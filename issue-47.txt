2.5 Liquidity Cost Allocation to Deferred Redemptions (NEW CALCULATION)
Current:

Swing pricing receivable is calculated on cash amounts
Cost allocation is: cost_per_unit = total_cost / total_cash_redemptions
Applied to deferred cash: deferred_swing_receivable = cost_per_unit * deferred_cash
This is WRONG because deferred units don't get costs allocated to them
New:

Liquidity costs recognized in redemption month apply only to executed redemptions
Deferred redemption units do NOT receive cost allocation in the deferral month

deferred_units_executed: Decimal,
nav_at_deferral: Decimal,
current_nav: Decimal,
total_cost: Decimal,
total_redemption_units: Decimal,

cost_to_deferred = cost_per_unit * deferred_units_executed

Deferred investors remain invested, so they don't pay costs in the deferral month

Before implementing the attached plan, apply these corrections.

Critical accounting correction:
Deferred redemption units remain economically invested until execution. Therefore, deferred units must not reduce investor ownership in the deferral month.

Correct formula:
closing_units = opening_units - paid_redemption_units

Incorrect formula to avoid:
closing_units = opening_units - paid_redemption_units - deferred_redemption_units

Deferred_redemption_units are pending redemption instructions held in backlog. They only reduce investor units when actually executed/paid in a later month.

Backlog valuation rule:
- Store backlog as units.
- Store nav_at_deferral for audit and later analysis.
- For current month exposure, backlog_cash_value = remaining_units * current_nav.
- Do not turn deferred units into fixed cash claims using nav_at_deferral.
- Unless existing product logic explicitly freezes the execution NAV, execution should happen at the NAV of the execution month.

Implementation scope control:
Do not rewrite the whole engine into a pure unit-led fund accounting system if not needed. It is acceptable for redemption demand and liquidation sizing to remain cash-equivalent calculations. The essential fix is that any unpaid/deferred portion is carried forward as units, not fixed cash.

Cost allocation rule:
Deferred units do not receive swing-pricing or liquidity-cost allocation in the month of deferral. Costs apply only to executed redemptions. If deferred units execute later, cost treatment belongs to the execution month.

Refs #47 convert deferred redemptions to unit-based backlog
